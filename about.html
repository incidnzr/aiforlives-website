Who We Are

AIforLives is a mission‑driven, social entrepreneurial not‑for‑profit enterprise focused on mobilizing and utilizing AI technologies to create social value.

Our Mission

  • To empower young entrepreneurs to transform ideas into real‑world solutions
  • To promote ethical and socially responsible use of AI
  • To create learning and collaboration opportunities through international projects
  • To support innovation in media, health, and environmental sustainability

Our Vision

A world where artificial intelligence is accessible, inclusive, and used responsibly to improve lives and protect our planet.

We believe that artificial intelligence should not be limited to commercial use—it should actively contribute to solving social, environmental, and human‑centered challenges.

Our Values

Guiding principles that shape everything we do

Social Impact

We measure success by the positive change we create in communities and lives.

Gender Equality & Inclusion

We actively work to empower young women and create equal opportunities in tech.

Innovation with Responsibility

We push technological boundaries while ensuring ethical and sustainable practices.

Collaboration & Knowledge Sharing

We believe in the power of partnerships and open exchange of ideas.

Our Story

AIforLives was founded on the belief that artificial intelligence holds unprecedented potential to address some of society's most pressing challenges. What began as an initiative to support young tech entrepreneurs has evolved into a comprehensive platform for social innovation.

Our work supports young changemakers, with a strong emphasis on empowering young women, to design and implement impactful, ethical, and sustainable AI‑based initiatives. Through Erasmus+ projects and international collaborations, we've created a network of innovators across Europe and beyond.

Our Approach

How we turn ideas into impact

01

Identify & Empower

We identify talented young entrepreneurs, particularly young women, and provide them with the resources, mentorship, and skills needed to develop their AI-based social innovations.

02

Develop & Prototype

Through workshops, hackathons, and co-creation labs, we help transform ideas into working prototypes, ensuring they address real community needs.

03

Implement & Scale

We support implementation through Erasmus+ projects and partnerships, helping solutions reach communities and create measurable impact.

04

Sustain & Evolve

We establish sustainable models for ongoing impact and continuously evolve our approach based on lessons learned and emerging challenges.
