Projects & Erasmus+

AIforLives develops and participates in national and international projects that combine education, innovation, and social impact.

Erasmus+ Projects

A key part of our work involves Erasmus+ projects, where we collaborate with partners across Europe and beyond. Through Erasmus+ programs, we provide non‑formal education and training, support youth exchanges and capacity‑building activities, promote intercultural dialogue and collaboration, and equip young people with AI, digital, and entrepreneurial skills.
