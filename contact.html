Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our work and how to get involved

At AI for Lives, we specialize in AI development, web applications, machine learning, and data analytics. We also offer professional web development services to help businesses build modern, responsive websites. Additionally, we collaborate on Erasmus+ projects across sectors like education, sport, and youth development.

Our services are specifically tailored to create social impact, with a focus on empowering young entrepreneurs and addressing challenges in media, health, and environmental sustainability.

We actively participate in Erasmus+ projects and are open to collaborations. You can reach out to us through our contact form with details about your organization and project idea. We typically look for partners who share our commitment to social impact and innovation.

Our Erasmus+ collaborations often involve:

  • Joint project proposals and applications
  • Knowledge exchange and capacity building
  • Youth exchanges and training programs
  • Research and development partnerships

We'll discuss how we can work together on future initiatives and explore funding opportunities.

Yes! We tailor AI-powered solutions and provide custom web development services to meet the specific needs of your business or organization. Whether it's automating processes, data analysis, app development, or building a professional website, we customize our services to fit your goals.

Our approach includes:

  • Needs assessment and consultation
  • Custom AI model development and training
  • Responsive web design and development
  • Integration with existing systems
  • Training and ongoing support

All our business solutions maintain our commitment to ethical AI and social responsibility.

There are several ways for young people to get involved:

  • Training Programs: Join our workshops on AI, digital skills, and social entrepreneurship
  • Erasmus+ Projects: Participate in youth exchanges and international collaborations
  • Mentorship: Receive guidance on developing your own AI-based social initiative
  • Volunteering: Contribute to ongoing projects and gain hands-on experience
  • Hackathons & Events: Join our innovation challenges and networking events

We particularly encourage applications from young women and underrepresented groups in tech.

Gender equality is a core value at AI for Lives. We actively work to:

  • Ensure at least 50% participation of women in all our programs and projects
  • Provide targeted mentorship and support for young women in AI and tech
  • Develop initiatives specifically addressing challenges faced by women and girls
  • Promote women leadership in AI and social innovation
  • Collaborate with organizations focused on gender equality

Our Score Women project (Erasmus+ Sport) is one example of our commitment to empowering women through sports and technology.
